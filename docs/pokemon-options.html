lzr1 Pokémon agents: how it would look

Idea from herdr-pokemon-agents: every session is a pixel-art Pokémon whose behavior follows the session's state. Working walks, waiting stops and calls with a pulsing !, done shows ✓, idle strolls and, after 3 minutes, sleeps (zZz). Everything here uses the panel's own cards and colors, and the browser strip uses the gateway's palette from client.js. The sessions are sample values (the same as the sidebar page) and the sprites are hand-drawn stand-ins; the real ones would come from PMD SpriteCollab at run time. Click a session or a Pokémon to select it, use the buttons to change its state, and open the picker in C to change its Pokémon. Every mockup follows.
